# Base44 Compared

Base44 pricing decoded: five tiers from 0 to 160 dollars a month, two separate credit pools, and the question of whether you need a platform or just the files.

**Read the full page:** https://base44-pricing.github.io/

Pick Base44 if the thing you are building has to keep running: users signing in, data persisting, payments collected, analytics accumulating. Apps built there run on production infrastructure rather than a sandbox, and platforms like that are priced as subscriptions because they keep working after you close the tab. Pick Begin.sh if what you want is the artefact, a static site or an Expo app you download as a zip and own outright, with no backend, no auth and no monthly bill. The caveat on the Base44 side is the credit model: two separate pools, message and integration, and consumption that varies with the action and the AI model you pick, so the tier you need is rarely the one you first estimate.
